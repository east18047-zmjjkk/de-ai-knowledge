# Before / After Examples

## English — General

**Before**:  
In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape, it is important to note that leveraging robust tools can unlock a transformative experience that underscores the pivotal role of technology.

**After**:  
Good tools make the work easier. The best ones stay out of the way so you can focus on what you're actually doing.

## English — Academic

**Before**:  
This study delves into the intricate relationship between variables. The findings underscore a comprehensive need for further research in this realm. Furthermore, the results play a crucial role in fostering understanding.

**After**:  
We examined how the two variables move together. The association is real but modest. More work is needed to separate cause from selection effects.

## English — Narrative

**Before**:  
Her heart hammered as she embarked on a journey into the unknown. The tension was palpable, a rich tapestry of old fears. It wasn't just fear; it was something deeper.

**After**:  
She stood at the platform edge longer than she meant to. The train was already there. She stepped on anyway, one hand still on the strap of her bag.

## Chinese — 通用

**Before**:  
在当今快速发展的时代，值得注意的是，这项技术赋能了用户，构建了全新的生态，说白了就是带来了革命性的改变。

**After**:  
这项技术让用户少做了几步操作。具体变在哪里，还要看实际用下来的反馈。

## Style-matched (direct, short sentences)

**Before**:  
It is worth noting that the new process will leverage existing infrastructure to streamline operations.

**After**:  
The new process uses what we already have. It should cut a couple of steps. We'll know in two weeks if it actually does.
