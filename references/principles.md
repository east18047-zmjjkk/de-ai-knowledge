# Core Principles for Removing AI Flavor

## Information Conservation
- Do not add unsupported facts, numbers, examples, or conclusions.
- Do not delete core arguments, necessary qualifications, or required structure.
- Tone strength should stay the same (e.g., “possible” does not become “certain”).

## Minimal Necessary Change
- Fix only what creates machine feel.
- If a sentence is already natural, leave it.
- Prefer deletion and substitution over full rewrite when meaning is intact.

## Human Interval, Not Anti-AI
- Human writing sits in the middle: occasional formal transitions, occasional contrast structures, occasional similar-length sentences.
- Zeroing out every tell creates a different fingerprint.
- Select 3–5 highest-impact issues per piece rather than applying every rule at once.

## Genre Awareness
- Academic: precision and formality are appropriate; robotic hedging and empty significance inflation are not.
- Narrative: showing, concrete action, and rhythm matter more than emotional labeling.
- Match the original register unless the user asks otherwise.

## Style Profile (when available)
- Prefer matching the user’s historical formality, rhythm, vocabulary, and directness.
- Do not invent a personality the samples do not support.
- Adapt within the profile’s range when genre differs from samples.

## Diagnostic Heuristic
Ask: “Would a careful human writer in this context produce this exact phrasing at this density?”
If the answer is no and the pattern is clustered, revise.
