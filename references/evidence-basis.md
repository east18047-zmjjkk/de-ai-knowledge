# Evidence Basis and Sources

This knowledge base is a practical compilation, not a single large-scale peer-reviewed corpus study. Items are graded by strength of public observation.

## Evidence Levels

| Level | Meaning | How to treat |
|-------|---------|--------------|
| A — High consensus | Repeatedly listed across multiple independent humanizer guides, detector feature discussions, and practitioner audits (2024–2026). Strong observational signal. | Flag when clustered; safe to revise. |
| B — Common observation | Appears in several public lists or model-output audits. Less quantified. | Flag when dense or formulaic; single natural use is fine. |
| C — Caution | Mentioned but also occurs in careful human writing; risk of over-correction. | Note only if extreme density; do not auto-rewrite. |

## Primary Public Sources Consulted

- Practitioner lists of AI words/phrases to avoid (e.g., Tracy Sheen “AI Words to Avoid”, PureWrite, WriteHuman, SEO Francisco, AIPromptIndex compilations, 2025–2026).
- Structural pattern discussions: “not X but Y” / negative parallelism, uniform sentence length / low burstiness, throat-clearing openers, forced triads (multiple humanizer and detector-related write-ups).
- Wikipedia “Signs of AI writing” style editor guidance references (commonly cited in humanizer prompts).
- EQ-Bench and similar detector feature weight discussions (contrast patterns, overused vocabulary).
- Direct observation of model outputs across common English academic and narrative prompts.

## Limitations

- No original large-scale human-vs-AI frequency counts were run for this skill.
- Model behavior shifts; high-frequency tells from 2024–early 2025 may decline while new ones appear.
- Academic and literary human writing legitimately uses some formal connectors and longer sentences; density and clustering matter more than single occurrences.
- Detection lists are diagnostic aids, not ground truth.

## Recommendation

Prefer Level A items for automatic attention. Use the detection checklist for systematic review. When in doubt, preserve meaning and register over aggressive “de-AI” rewriting.
